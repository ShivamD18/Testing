Updating your week

Send Claude your week in plain words. Claude saves it here, and the planner builds the schedule around your fixed times.

  1. Sunday: send the full week: classes, labs, shifts and everything due, with hours and a priority from 1 to 5.
  2. During the week: tell Claude what changed, for example "essay is now due Wednesday" or "add a quiz Friday, 1 hr, priority 2".
  3. As you work: tick sessions off here. Anything you don't finish rolls forward on its own.

Work can overlap class times but never labs or work shifts. Each task is planned to finish 1 day before it's due (2 days for priority 5).
